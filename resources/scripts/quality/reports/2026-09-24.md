## Limits and next step

This establishes equivalence for one real recording, not for the entire catalog.
The database was live, not a frozen snapshot. Redis dispatch and heartbeat are
covered by the separate integration tests; this probe exercises recognition
without running Redis/Kafka consumers. Sampled heap usage is not a maximum RSS
measurement or a guarantee against OOM on larger inputs.

Next step: deploy the tested candidate to one Redis worker, retain two windows,
3.5 GiB heap and 4.5 GiB container memory, and observe real task completions,
reclaims, errors, and memory before expanding deployment.
